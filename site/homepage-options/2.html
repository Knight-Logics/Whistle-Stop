Written in chalk since 1995

This corner started as Frostee Harbor, Jane and Henry Fox's drive-in ice cream stand. Dawn fell for it in 1995 and bought it twice, the second time with Patrick in 2000.

The take-out window grew into 100-plus seats, a full bar and an open-air patio. Henry's milkshakes never left the menu.
