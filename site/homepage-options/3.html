“Since 1995, I have had a true romance with this place. Purchasing it not once, but twice!”

Before it was Whistle Stop, this corner was Frostee Harbor, Jane and Henry Fox's drive-in ice cream stand, for more than 35 years. Henry's famous milkshakes are still on the menu. Today there are 100-plus seats, a full bar and 18 taps.
