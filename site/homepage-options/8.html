From Frostee Harbor to Whistle Stop

People still stop by to tell us about coming here “back in the day.” Here's how the corner got from a take-out window to Main Street's grill and bar.

  1. The drive-in yearsJane and Henry Fox run Frostee Harbor, an ice cream stand with burgers, fries and Henry's milkshakes, for more than 35 years.
  2. 1995Dawn buys the place for the first time.
  3. 2000She buys it again with her husband Patrick, and together they build Whistle Stop Grill & Bar.
  4. Today100-plus seats, a full bar, 18 taps and an open-air patio. The milkshakes stayed.
