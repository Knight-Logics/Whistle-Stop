The sign's been on since the drive-in days

Before it was Whistle Stop, this corner was Frostee Harbor, Jane and Henry Fox's drive-in ice cream stand, a Main Street landmark for more than 35 years. Henry's milkshakes are still on our menu.

Dawn bought the place in 1995, then bought it again with her husband Patrick in 2000. Together they turned a take-out window into 100-plus seats, a full bar and an open-air patio.
